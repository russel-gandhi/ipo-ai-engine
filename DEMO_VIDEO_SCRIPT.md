# IPO-AI Demo Video Script (2 mins 30 secs)

**Target Duration**: 2 minutes 15 seconds to 2 minutes 45 seconds  
**Target Audience**: Hackathon Judges, Investors, SEBI Market Participants  
**Key Value Proposition**: Replacing misleading subscription multiple rules-of-thumb with regulatorily-defensible IPO allotment analytics and authoritative data provenance.

---

## Script Breakdown

### Segment 1: Problem Statement (0:00 - 0:15)
- **Screen Action**: Open browser to Landing Page (`http://localhost:3000`). Hero text "IPO Allotment & Listing Analytics Engine" is visible.
- **Mouse Action**: Hover cursor over the search bar and recent IPO cards.
- **Voiceover**: 
  > *"Every week, millions of retail investors apply for IPOs in India. But almost everyone relies on a dangerous myth—assuming that a 10x share subscription multiple means they have a 10% chance of allotment. In reality, SEBI draw-of-lots algorithms depend on application-level competition, not total shares bid."*
- **Visible Text**: `"IPO Allotment & Listing Analytics Engine — Designed to Apply SEBI & Exchange Framework"`

---

### Segment 2: Product Overview (0:15 - 0:30)
- **Screen Action**: Scroll past live IPO cards (*Juniper Green Energy*, *G.V. Electricals*, *H.R. Hygiene Products*).
- **Mouse Action**: Click on *Juniper Green Energy* card to navigate to detail view (`/analyse/juniper-green-energy`).
- **Voiceover**: 
  > *"Meet IPO-AI—the first Indian IPO intelligence platform that strictly separates demand multiples from allotment probability and resolves regulatory rules dynamically under SEBI ICDR guidelines."*
- **Visible Text**: `Juniper Green Energy | Mainboard | Book-Built | NSE & BSE`

---

### Segment 3: Core IPO Intelligence & Data Provenance (0:30 - 1:00)
- **Screen Action**: Show Issue Overview, Normalized Financial Period Tags (FY2023, FY2024), Offer Breakdown, and Subscription Dashboard.
- **Mouse Action**: Hover over Retail Quota tooltip (*"% of Net Offer to Public"*), then hover over the Subscription Dashboard timestamp (*"As of 02 Aug 2026, 16:00 IST"*).
- **Voiceover**: 
  > *"Here on the detail page, every date is tagged as Confirmed or Tentative, financial periods distinguish Revenue from Operations, and quota percentages explicitly state their denominators. Live subscription demand is displayed factually with exact timestamps."*
- **Visible Text**: `"Subscription Dashboard — As of 02 Aug 2026, 16:00 IST"`

---

### Segment 4: The Allotment Engine & Safe Incomplete-Data State (1:00 - 1:40)
- **Screen Action**: Scroll down to the **SEBI Allotment Calculator**. Select Retail tab.
- **Mouse Action**: Click "RETAIL" tab, then click **"Calculation Methodology & SEBI Regulations"** to expand the drawer.
- **Voiceover**: 
  > *"When we open the Allotment Calculator for a live issue like Juniper Green Energy, notice what happens: instead of fabricating fake odds from the 3.2x share subscription, the engine honestly states 'Exact Allotment Odds Unavailable—valid application-count data is not yet published by the registrar.' Expanding the Calculation Methodology drawer shows the exact SEBI ICDR Regulation reference, required vs available inputs, and audit trace."*
- **Visible Text**: 
  - Header: `"Exact Allotment Odds Unavailable"`
  - Status: `"INSUFFICIENT_APPLICATION_DATA"`
  - Regulation: `"SEBI (ICDR) Regulations 2018, Schedule XIII, Part A"`

---

### Segment 5: Official Basis of Allotment (Post-Allotment State) (1:40 - 2:10)
- **Screen Action**: Navigate to historical demo IPO *Zaggle Prepaid Ocean Services* (`/analyse/zaggle-prepaid-ocean-services`).
- **Mouse Action**: Click on *Zaggle Prepaid Ocean Services* search result or direct link. Scroll to Allotment Calculator and expand Methodology drawer.
- **Voiceover**: 
  > *"Now watch what happens when we switch to a historical issue like Zaggle Prepaid Ocean Services where the registrar's official Basis of Allotment has been published. The engine ingests authoritative KFintech filing data, transitions to 'FINAL BASIS OF ALLOTMENT', and reveals the true retail allotment ratio of 16.67%—or 1 in 6 successful applications."*
- **Visible Text**: 
  - Status Badge: `"FINAL BASIS OF ALLOTMENT"`
  - Probability: `"16.67%"`
  - Source: `"KFin Technologies Limited Basis of Allotment"`

- **Voiceover**:
